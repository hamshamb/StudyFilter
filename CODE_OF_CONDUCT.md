# Code of conduct

StudyFilter is built for learners. Project spaces should therefore be patient, welcoming, and safe for contributors of every background and experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and explain decisions clearly.
- Protect the privacy and safety of students and contributors.
- Avoid discriminatory, sexual, threatening, or harassing language and behaviour.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behaviour may be reported privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
